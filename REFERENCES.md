# References

Works cited across the report, the annotated notebook, and this repository's documentation. They
are grouped by the role they play here: the atmospheric-correction uncertainty literature the
method builds on, the retrieval and radiative-transfer work the tool sits downstream of, and the
published algorithms whose sensitivity the notebook measures.

`report/PROJECT_SUMMARY.md` keeps its own reference list, frozen with the submission and scoped to
the works that document cites. This file is the maintained, repository-wide superset.
